Get started

Before you start

You need an iPhone and a ChatGPT Plus plan (or higher). To have ChatGPT tap for you, add a small accessory and use a computer once to set it up.

Helping a family member set up? Use their own iPhone and ChatGPT account.

Set up on your iPhone

  1. Install the SaySo app on your iPhone

    Beta sign-up opens soon. We’ll post the TestFlight link here when it opens.

  2. Connect ChatGPT

    Tap “Connect ChatGPT” in the SaySo app.

    1. In ChatGPT, tap the model name, choose Work, and send the prefilled connection message. You’ll see a connection link.

      If ChatGPT installs the SaySo service first instead, wait for it to finish, return to the SaySo app, tap “Connect ChatGPT” again, and send the new message in a new Work conversation.

    2. Open the link ChatGPT gives you. Tap “Generate connection”, then “Return to SaySo to connect”.

      The SaySo app shows “Service connected”.

    The SaySo service is a private service set up in your own ChatGPT account. ChatGPT uses it to see your shared screen and, with an accessory, tap for you.

    Use Work’s tools to install and connect your private SaySo service. For everyday help, switch to Chat and start Voice in the conversation where you send the prefilled sharing message in step 3. To switch, tap the model name in ChatGPT and choose Work or Chat. How to switch

    Need help?

  3. Start sharing and ask

    1. In the SaySo app, tap “Start sharing”. If SaySo asks for consent, review where your screen goes, then tap “Agree and start sharing” if you agree.
    2. In the iOS window, check that it shows SaySo, tap “Start Broadcast”, and wait for the countdown. If the window doesn’t open, tap “Open broadcast window” in SaySo first.
    3. When sharing starts, tap “Open ChatGPT” in SaySo. In ChatGPT, tap the model name and choose Chat if needed. Send the prefilled message.
    4. In that conversation, start Voice.
    5. Switch to the app you want help with and ask your question.

    Using the accessory? If SaySo shows “Allow tapping”, tap it before you ask ChatGPT to tap.

    ChatGPT answers about the screen you’re on.

    Need help?

Want ChatGPT to tap for you? (optional)

Tapping for you requires an iPhone with USB-C (iPhone 15 or later).

  1. Set up the accessory on a computer

    Get an M5Stack AtomS3 Lite (C124) and a USB-C data cable. Open the accessory setup page in Chrome or Edge on your computer and install SaySo on the accessory so it can pair with your iPhone. Where to buy the accessory

    Set up the accessory

    The browser shows “All installed!”.

    Need help?

  2. Connect and pair

    Connect the accessory to a compatible iPhone with the USB-C data cable. When “SaySo accessory found” appears, tap “Start pairing”. When the light flashes, press the front button once. This confirms you’re pairing the accessory in your hand.

    The accessory shows as connected.

    Need help?

  3. The accessory needs AssistiveTouch to tap the screen

    In Settings, search for “AssistiveTouch”. Turn on AssistiveTouch. To drag, also turn on “Perform Touch Gestures” on the same screen.

    AssistiveTouch instructions

    In SaySo, tap “Open Settings”.

    1. If Settings is on the SaySo page, go back to the main Settings screen first.
    2. Tap “Accessibility”.
    3. Tap “Touch” to find the touch and pointer controls.
    4. Tap “AssistiveTouch” to open its settings.
    5. Turn on “AssistiveTouch” so the accessory can tap the screen.
    6. For dragging, scroll down on the same screen and turn on “Perform Touch Gestures”. This lets the accessory drag items like a finger.
    7. Optional: Tap “Pointer Style”, then “Color”, and choose a color you can easily see. You can keep the default size. Labels may vary by iOS version.

    Back in SaySo, tap “I’ve turned on AssistiveTouch”.

    Need help?
In ChatGPT: app permissions (optional)

In ChatGPT, open Settings, go to Apps (or Plugins if that’s what your menu shows), select SaySo, and open Permissions. If available, choose “Allow all actions”. Some accounts show “Allow all tools” or “Allow all”. This lets ChatGPT use SaySo for tapping with fewer approval prompts. For payments, orders, sending and deleting, ChatGPT is told to leave the final tap to you. You can tap “Stop tapping” in the SaySo app at any time while tapping is allowed.

In SaySo: how to allow tapping

You can change this in Settings.

Allow automatically
Turns on tapping automatically each time you start sharing when your accessory is ready.
Ask me each time
After you start sharing, tap “Allow tapping” in SaySo when you want help.

For payments, orders, sending and deleting, ChatGPT is told to leave the final tap to you. Tap “Stop tapping” in SaySo whenever tapping is on.

ChatGPT is told to stop at the final button and let you tap it. It usually does, but this isn’t guaranteed, so check before every tap.

Costs and free sessions

Letting ChatGPT see your screen is free with SaySo. SaySo’s free plan includes 10 tapping sessions each month. A session starts when ChatGPT first taps for you. It lasts up to 30 minutes and ends after 15 minutes of no activity. Tapping after that starts a new session. SaySo Pro is available monthly or annually. To see prices, open Settings in the SaySo app and choose “Subscription plans” under SaySo Pro. Your ChatGPT subscription and accessory are separate purchases.

Troubleshooting

When ChatGPT asks for permission

Tap “Allow” or “Decline” on the card. Saying “yes” doesn’t count.

Connecting to ChatGPT doesn’t share your screen automatically. Tap “Start sharing” in SaySo when you’re ready.

Before SaySo types for you, switch your keyboard to English (ABC). For now, it can only type English letters and numbers.

How is this different from ChatGPT’s built-in screen sharing?

With SaySo, ChatGPT can see your iPhone screen while you use other apps. With the accessory, it can also tap, type or scroll for you.
