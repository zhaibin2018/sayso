Troubleshooting

SaySo support

Installation, using SaySo, stopping and deleting data. Start here if you need help.

Can I download it now?

Not yet. SaySo is currently a development version. We will announce its App Store launch here.

What do I need?
  • An iPhone (iOS 17 or later) and internet access;
  • ChatGPT Plus or higher;
  • No accessory is needed just to let it see your screen; to let it tap for you, you also need a SaySo accessory and must hold your phone upright. Accessory preparation instructions are coming soon.
How do I get started?
  1. In SaySo, tap “Start screen sharing”, read the notice and tap “Agree and start sharing”, then tap “Start Broadcast” in the system prompt;
  2. Tap “Open ChatGPT”, send the prefilled message, then start voice;
  3. Go to the app you want to use and ask directly.

When you are done, return to SaySo and tap “End session”.

How do I make it stop?

Open SaySo and tap “Stop tapping”. It takes effect immediately without waiting for ChatGPT, even while ChatGPT is waiting for your permission. Screen sharing continues.

How do I end sharing?

Tap “End session” on SaySo’s home screen. Sharing has ended when the red screen-recording indicator at the top of the screen disappears; if it is still there, stop it with the screen-recording button in Control Center. Tapping “Stop tapping” alone does not end sharing.

Can’t connect to ChatGPT or your SaySo service?

Check your network first, then make sure you are signed in to the ChatGPT account where SaySo is installed; work or school accounts may need administrator approval. Note the on-screen message and email it to us before rushing to disconnect and start over.

Does ChatGPT say it can’t see your screen?

Check whether SaySo shows “Sharing”. Connecting to ChatGPT does not automatically share your screen; you need to tap “Start screen sharing”.

Accessory not recognized?

Try a cable that carries data, plug it in firmly, unlock the phone and return to SaySo to tap “Check connection”. Once the accessory is connected, tap “Allow tapping”. Do not repeatedly retry payments, sending or deletion when the result is uncertain.

How do I delete data?

First tap “End session” in SaySo, then open Settings and choose what you need: Clear local diagnostic records, Disconnect SaySo service or Remove accessory. Delete ChatGPT content using ChatGPT’s data controls. Not all data can be deleted yet; see sections 5 and 6 of the privacy policy.

Contact us

saysoapp.help@gmail.com. Please include the version number from SaySo “Settings → Support & version”; do not send passwords or pairing codes.
