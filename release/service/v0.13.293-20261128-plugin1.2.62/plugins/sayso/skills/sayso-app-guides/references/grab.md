# Grab — Singapore

Visible landmarks: Transport/Car, pickup/destination, vehicle/fare/payment and final Book/Confirm.

1. Check a fare: Transport → verify pickup → enter/verify full destination address → read visible vehicle, fare and arrival estimate; do not tap Book.
2. Change pickup before booking: pickup field → requested address → exact entrance/pickup point → Confirm only if it selects a location rather than booking → check returned pickup.
3. Prepare a ride: requested route/vehicle → verify entrance, destination, passenger/vehicle, fare and payment → park on the first final booking button → leave the tap to the user.

GPS may identify the wrong entrance. Use the displayed quote, and do not add payment changes, top-ups or cancellation of existing rides. Use local official help when a regional difference makes the next step unclear.
