# Contributions

External code, documentation, translation and asset contributions are not being accepted at this stage. Please do not submit patches for merging. Issues, reproducible bug reports and general feedback are welcome; do not include private device data, credentials, screenshots containing personal information or code you do not have permission to disclose.

This policy does not transfer anyone's copyright. A future contribution process may require a separately reviewed contributor agreement before accepting changes. No CLA is implied by filing an issue, opening a pull request, viewing or forking this repository.

Third-party dependency updates keep their original licenses and attribution. The maintainer must not describe third-party work as owned by the SaySo licensor merely because it appears in this repository.
