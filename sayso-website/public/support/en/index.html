Troubleshooting

SaySo support

Installation, using SaySo, stopping and deleting data. Start here if you need help.

What do I need?
  • An iPhone (iOS 17 or later) and internet access;
  • ChatGPT Plus or higher;
  • ChatGPT can see your screen on Lightning or USB-C iPhones without an accessory. Tapping for you requires a USB-C iPhone (iPhone 15 or later), a SaySo accessory and portrait mode.

See Set up the accessory for setup.

How do I get started?

Open Get started to install the SaySo app and connect ChatGPT.

  1. In the SaySo app, tap “Start sharing”. If SaySo asks for consent, review where your screen goes, then tap “Agree and start sharing” if you agree.
  2. In the iOS window, check that it shows SaySo, tap “Start Broadcast”, and wait for the countdown. If the window doesn’t open, tap “Open broadcast window” in SaySo first.
  3. When sharing starts, tap “Open ChatGPT” in SaySo. In ChatGPT, tap the model name and choose Chat if needed. Send the prefilled message.
  4. In that conversation, start Voice.
  5. Switch to the app you want help with and ask your question.

Using the accessory? If SaySo shows “Allow tapping”, tap it before you ask ChatGPT to tap.

When you’re done, tap “End sharing” on the SaySo home screen.

How do I make it stop?

Open SaySo and tap “Stop tapping”. It takes effect immediately without waiting for ChatGPT, even while ChatGPT is waiting for your permission. Screen sharing continues.

How do I end sharing?

Tap “End sharing” on SaySo’s home screen and look for “Sharing ended”. You can also check that the red screen-recording indicator at the top of the screen has disappeared. If it is still there, stop recording in Control Center. Tapping “Stop tapping” alone does not end sharing.

Can’t connect to ChatGPT or your SaySo service?

The SaySo service is a private service set up in your own ChatGPT account. ChatGPT uses it to see your shared screen and, with an accessory, tap for you. Check your network first, then make sure you’re signed in to the ChatGPT account where the SaySo service is installed. Work or school accounts may need administrator approval. Before you disconnect and start over, email us the message on screen. It helps us find the cause.

Does ChatGPT say it can’t see your screen?

Check whether SaySo shows “Sharing”. Connecting to ChatGPT does not automatically share your screen; you need to tap “Start sharing”.

Accessory not recognized?

Try a USB-C data cable and unlock the phone so SaySo can detect the accessory. In SaySo, open Settings → SaySo accessory and tap “Check connection”. Once the accessory is connected, tap “Allow tapping”.

See Set up the accessory.

How do I delete data?

First tap “End sharing” in SaySo, then open Settings and choose what you need: Clear local diagnostic records, Disconnect SaySo service or Remove accessory. Delete ChatGPT content using ChatGPT’s data controls. Not all data can be deleted yet; see sections 5 and 6 of the privacy policy.

Unsure whether a payment, message or deletion went through?

Check the result in the app before you try again. Avoid repeated attempts while the result is unclear.

ChatGPT is told to stop at the final button and let you tap it. It usually does, but this isn’t guaranteed, so check before every tap.
